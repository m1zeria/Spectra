# Spectra

*A story about multitudes.*

There is a city that runs on clockwork and forgetting. 
The old gods are still here, in the heart of every machine. 

Somewhere beneath the streets, the old machinery turns. 
Gears align and shift, and so do connections. The copper 
veins beneath every road flicker with ancient power.
The electric hum that never fades was once a hymn.

You wake up in this city. You do not know how you got here. 
You do not know why the street feels familiar. You do not 
know the name of the person who is speaking to you, or why 
they are looking at you like they have known you for years.

You are not alone in your own mind.

---

**Spectra** is a branching visual novel about identity,
found family, grief and the things better left unspoken.
It is set in a post-apocalyptic steampunk world where 
the apocalypse was not fire or flood, but one caused by
the relentless greed of mankind, one where the old gods 
are still being harvested, one relic at a time.
