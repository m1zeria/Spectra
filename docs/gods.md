# Gods

They were the rulers once, and they remember; they are still here, living in the machines.

The gods speak an ancient language with no name that can be spoken by human tongues. It appears as garbled text in game, and reading it is incredibly taxing on the reader, because it is too much to comprehend. The gods converse in it.

They know they're on the same boat, but they do not agree on what to do about it and fight because they disagree on how to restore themselves to former glory.

## The Seven Bands

The pantheon, as the street calls it. The Tuned call them the Harrowed, the Body calls them assets, and the oldest of the old call them by their old names in secret.

## Names and Registers

Every god has five names.

- A Greek root-name — formal, temple use
- A Fancy Epithet — poetic, feared, used in oaths
- A Latin ceremonial title — legal, the Artificer's Guild
- A Street name — what the common folk mutter
- A True-Name syllable — whispered only by cultists, incomplete

Which register you use tells people who you are.

## Collective Titles

- The Seven Bands — street
- The Chromatic Court — formal, political
- The Refracted Ones — theological, the fall from wholeness
- The Luminous Synod — church
- The Colorless Throne — what they sit upon
- The First Refraction — the act that created them
- The Harrowed — Seer word, not used by anyone else

## The Worshipped and the Denied

Each god has an orthodox aspect and a denied aspect. The Body prefers
the orthodox. The undercity prefers the denied.

## Heretical Additions

### The White

The hypothetical eighth god. Sum of all light. The one whose coming
would unmake the spectrum — or reunite it, depending on who is asked.

### The Blind One

No band. No color. Absolute absence. The anti-god. It sponsors the
Body. It wants the extermination of the Seven and the awakening of
certain dark things. It fears the protagonist.

### The Unrefracted

The primordial source, before the gods split. The truth the Body is
covering up. The thing the Chromaticity was before it became the
Chromaticity.
