## Themes

### Core

- Being many in a world that wants one.
- Who you are when you aren't in control.
- The people who choose you back.
- Grief for what was lost, and what isn't allowed.
- Losing humanity in a world overrun by machines,
  both literal and metaphorical.
- The handmade and meaningful against the 
  manufactured and mass produced.

### Secondary

- Faith and doubt as survival.
- Inheritance — blood, curse, debt.
- The body and mind as contested ground.
- Decay as transformation.
- The horrifying as the beautiful.
- The city as a body.

### What this story refuses

- Abuse dressed as romance.
- Illness as villainy.
- Disorders as twist, punchline, or horror.
- The System winning without cost.
