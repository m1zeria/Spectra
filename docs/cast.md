### 1. The Lamplighter's Apprentice

**Name**: Lumen Brightwell

**Occupation**: Apprentice to the municipal lamplighters — the guild of workers who tend the streetlamps and public prisms of Lumenspire. Officially, they are city employees. Unofficially, they are the closest thing the god Phaos has to priests.

**Age**: 15

**Story**: They were chosen by Phaos himself, not through any grand revelation, but through a quiet moment in a dark alley when a streetlamp flickered on at exactly the right moment and saved them from something hiding in the shadows. The Lamplighter's Apprentice carries a splinter of Phaos's divine light in their chest, a warmth that never fades, and they can sense when a lamp is about to fail, when a prism is misaligned, when the light is wrong. They are being slowly groomed to become the next Lamplighter — the mortal vessel who keeps Phaos anchored to the city.

**Abilities**: Can communicate with Phaos through flickering lights. Can sense the presence of the other gods' influence. Their touch can purify corrupted light. They do not cast a shadow — a detail that unnerves everyone who notices.

**Conflict**: The Body wants to study them. Phaos wants to protect them. And the Lamplighter's Apprentice is beginning to realize that their god's gentle, submissive nature may be a mask for something far older and far more terrifying.

**Appearance**: Long, silvery hair and grey eyes with an unnerving shimmer to them. Soot-stained fingers, brass goggles, and a coat covered in pinpricks of light that never go out.

### 2. The Radio Static

**Name**: Orpheus Quill

**Occupation**: A courier and information broker who operates in the space betweenthe gods' domains. He is one of the Muted, the cultists of Phonos who have taken vows of silence. But he cheated, and communicates through aetheric telegraphy. He taps messages into the city's forgotten infrastructure, sending signals through pipes and cables that even the body cannot monitor.

**Age**: 34

**Story**: Once a promising scholar at the Artificers' Guild, he discovered that the gods' supposedly unreadable language is in fact decipherable. It is encoded. He has spent the last decode trying to decode it, and he is close. He knows Phonos can still speak, but only in the language of static. He is trying to build a device that can translate it. The Body would kill him if it knew, even Phonos could. But he is still too useful to too many people, and he survives.

**Abilities**: He can hear aetheric transmissions nobody can perceive. He can send messages through any conductive material. He cannot speak, for his vocal cords were severed in a ritual. But he has learned that silence is a language of its own.

**Conflict**: He is being hunted by the Muted, who considers him a heretic for attempting to speak for Phonos. He is being courted by the Tuned, who want to use his decoding abilities. He is slowly falling in love with a blind priestess of the Unmade, who cannot see him or talk to him.

**Appearance**: Tall, always wearing a large coat with an upturned collar to hide the copper wires coiling his body and leading to his communication device. Brown hair and dark eyes.

### 3. The Ossuary's Curator

**Name**: Mortimer Röntgen

**Occupation**: The caretaker of the Ossuary Cathedral, the temple of Osteon. He is responsible for the collection, preservation and cataloguing of the bones of the faithful — a task that is both sacred and deeply macabre.

**Age**: 63

**Story**: He was a physician before he joined the Seers, a surgeon who specialised in bone diseases. He came to the Ossuary seeking answers to a question that had haunted him his entire career; why are some feelings truly bone-deep? Does the skeleton hold the answer to all that mystifies the scholar? He found his answer in Osteon. The god showed him the truth: in the cycle of life and death, in the regeneration of matter lies the secret to life. Death and decomposition is not an end, but a beginning. Mortimer is now consulted whenever old wisdom is needed.

**Abilities**: He can read the history of a person from their bones. He can see death; not as a looming threat, but as a quiet companion. He can see the "skeletons" of buildings, institutions, and societies, which makes him a valuable advisor to anyone who wants to know how long something will last before it collapses.

**Conflict**: He discovered a skeleton in the Ossuary's collection that should not exist — the bones of a god. He does not know which god, or how it died, but he knows that if the Body finds out, they will stop at nothing to acquire it. He is trying to determine the truth before the Body catches wind of his discovery.

**Appearance**: Long greying hair. Flowing black robes and a pendant shaped like vertebrae. His hands arealways cold, and his eyes are calm and knowing, as if they have already seen everything.

### 4. The Static Prophet

**Name**: Delphine "Desdaemona" Jacquard

**Occupation**: A rogue Artificer who has discovered a way to weaponize the gods' language. She can encode aetheric commands into everyday objects — a music box that can cause seizures, a telegraph key that can rewrite memories, a pocket watch that can stop a heart. The Body wants her dead. The Endwise want her converted.

**Age**: 18

**Story**: She was a prodigy in the Artificers' Guild, one of the youngest minds ever admitted to the inner circles. She was tasked with a simple project: find a way to make the Tithing more efficient. Instead, she found a way to make it obsolete. She discovered that the gods' language is not only a means of communication; it is a *programming language*. The gods are not just bound to machines; they are *code*, and code can be rewritten. She has been on the run ever since, trying to stay one step ahead of the Body while searching for a way to use their discovery to free the gods — or destroy them entirely. The streets call her Desdaemona, for the daemons she uploads to the aetheric cables are far more deadly than those of myth.

**Abilities**: She can encode aetheric commands into physical objects. She can "hack" into the city's infrastructure and temporarily redirect the gods' power. She can understand the garbled text of the gods' language without experiencing the usual debilitating effects — a trait that makes her unique and terrifying.

**Conflict**: No one knows she is the Guild's favourite, for she erased her old identity with evidence of her discovery. If they recovered their memories, then not only would she be captured, she would be sanitised like all others that do not align with the Body's restrictive standards.

**Appearance**: Lives in an RGB-blue cloak the same colour as her hair. Dark circles ring her golden eyes, and when spoken to she is endlessly wise if you look past her stutter.
