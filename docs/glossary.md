# Glossary

## The Body

The conglomerate's formal name. Also called the Upstairs. It has Parts: the
Eyes (spies), the Hands (enforcers), and others. It is atheist. It does
not know that it serves the Blind One.

## The Chorus

You, what the gods seem to call you.

## The Chromaticity

The truth about the gods. The phenomenon. The whole that was refracted.
Classified by the Body. Known by the Tuned. Embodied, unknowingly, by
the protagonist.

## The Core

Technical name for the Throne. See `world.md`.

## The Endwise

The Blind One's agents. A direction, not a faction. They serve absence.

## The Eyes

A Part of the Body. Spies, watchers, informants.

## The Hands

A Part of the Body. Enforcers.

## The Harrowed

A Seer word for the gods. Not used by anyone else. It means: those who
have been harvested.

## The Prism

What the protagonist is. A fragment of every god, walking around in a
single body. The Eighth. The Key. Also, depending on who you ask, the
White.

## The Seven Bands

The gods, collectively. Street name. The ones behind the machines. See `gods.md`.

## The Throne

The Colorless Throne. A metaphor and a real core. The source. In-world
people call it the Throne. Technical documents call it the Core.

## The Tithing

The process of harvesting a god for power. Church language applied to
industrial murder. The Body chose the word on purpose.

## The Tuned

The Seers. Self-name. The Body calls them cultists. They can hear the
gods. They hold meetings with them. They are killed, worked, or made
examples of, when they interfere.

## Upstairs

Street name for the Body. Capital U optional. Everyone knows what it
means.
