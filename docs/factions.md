# Factions

## The Body

A conglomerate of many corporations, operating as a single organism.
The people call it Upstairs. It has Parts: the Eyes are spies, the Hands
are enforcers, and there are others. The Body is shadowy, but it is
ingrained into everything. It controls the sale of every item whether
or not you like it.

The Body is atheist. It does not believe in gods. It harvests them
anyway, because the gods are resources, and resources are for
extraction. The Body does not know that the Blind One sponsors it, or
controls it, or watches it. It is very sure it is in charge.

### Parts

- **The Eyes** — spies, informants, watchers
- **The Hands** — enforcers, extraction, quiet work
- **Others** — TBD

### What the Body does to the different

Sanatoriums. Asylums. Extermination. Harvesting.

Anyone who is Different — disabled, Tuned, god-touched, off in a
way the Body doesn't like — is a problem. Problems should be solved.

## The Tuned

The Seers. The Body calls them cultists. They call themselves the Tuned,
because they have tuned in to a frequency the rest cannot hear.

The Tuned see that the gods are ostracized and used, just like them.
They call them the Harrowed.
They try to hold meetings with the gods. They organize, loosely. They
are killed, worked, or made examples of. Otherwise they are left alone.

Many of the protagonist's companions are the Tuned. Maybe even they are,
in their own way.
