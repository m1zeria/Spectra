## World

### City

Lumenspire. It has other names, names the Body uses
in this tedious bureaucracy, names the Tuned whisper
in temples, names used beyond the Paling where those
loyal to the Blind One lie in external prostration.

The city is sprawling but cramped, populated but silent
beyond the odd whir of a machine or the creak of a cog. 
Some do not believe it has a boundary, but it does — 
The Paling. Nothing leaves and, before The First 
Refraction, nothing entered either.

It is divided into scattered little districts, which
themselves form regions dominated by one of the
Chromatic Court. One would expect that such a city
be lively. It is quiet as death. You cannot change this.

### The Machinery

Clockwork is everywhere. Code, in a non-traditional
sense, is, too. Wires, gears, pipes, copper veins 
under the roads. The electric hum never stops. The
machinery is not only the city's infrastructure, but
its nervous system. Whoever controls the pipes controls
what flows through them. Which is to say: everything.

Most people interact with the machinery the way you interact 
with weather. It is there. It does what it does. You plan 
around it. Few but the best of the Artificers' Guild know 
how it works, for that too is gatekept by the Body.

The Body holds the center. The people do the labor.

### The Gods

They were the rulers once, and they remember; 
they are still here, living in the machines.

The gods speak an ancient language with no name that can be 
spoken by human tongues. It appears as garbled text in game, 
and reading it is incredibly taxing on the reader, because 
it is too much to comprehend. The gods converse in it.

They know they're on the same boat, but they do not agree 
on what to do about it and fight because they disagree
on how to restore themselves to former glory.

- **Light** submits. Willingly.
- **Gamma** massacres. Radiation, indiscriminate, unforgiving.
- **The others** have their own reactions, their own plans, 
    their own ways of addressing the conflict.

### The Tithing

The process of harvesting the gods for power. The radio 
god is farmed for radio waves. The light god is farmed for 
lightbulbs. Every utility in Lumenspire is a dismembered 
divinity, and the people who use it do not know, or do not 
think about it, or have been taught not to.

The Body calls it the Tithing. The Tuned call it the Plundering.
The word matters. It's church language applied to systemic
exploitation, and the Body chose it on purpose.

### The Chromaticity

The truth about the gods. The phenomenon. The whole that was 
refracted. The Body keeps it classified. The Tuned know it. 
The common people have heard the word and do not know what it 
means.

The Chromaticity is not a place. It is the thing the world was 
built on top of, and the thing the world was built to hide.

## The First Refraction

History says it was a war. The gods think it might have been. 
It may have been an amicable split. It may have been a forced 
separation. It may have been a choice.

The truth is contested. The Body exploits the contest. As long 
as the gods disagree about what happened, they can be played 
against each other.

## The Colorless Throne

A metaphor. Also a real core. The source of all power, from 
which everything stems. Something astronomical, something 
big-bang-adjacent. The gods sit upon it, metaphorically. 
The Body wants it. The Blind One is its absence.

In-world, people call it the Throne. Technical documents 
call it the Core. A separate document — `science.md` — 
will handle the cosmology.

## The Tone

Gloom and doom, mostly. Gothic steampunk post-apocalypse. The dark is
earned. The light is small and real, and it is the people who keep it
alive, because the Body has never figured out how to take human
connection away.
